Sponsorship

Every dollar becomes a part in a student’s hands.

We keep every lesson free for students and schools. Sponsors are the reason that is possible, and because we are a 501(c)(3), every contribution is tax deductible.

Become a sponsor
Where it goes

We spend on materials, not overhead.

We are volunteer run, so sponsorship money goes almost entirely into things students physically touch.

1

Parts and components

Arduino boards, breadboards, sensors, motors, gears, and filament. These are consumed or sent home with students, so they need restocking constantly.

2

Tools and equipment

3D printers, hand tools, multimeters, and the loaner laptops we bring to sites that do not have their own.

3

Reaching more schools

Transportation and printed materials, so we can say yes to schools further out rather than only the ones nearby.

Sponsorship levels

Pick a level, or tell us what works.

These are guidelines, not requirements. If none of them fit your budget or your company’s giving process, say so in the form and we will work with it.

$250+ Supporter
  • Funds materials for a classroom session
  • Logo listed on our site
  • Thank you in a group Instagram story
  • Thank you letter and tax receipt
  • Written report on what your funding reached
$1,000+ Builder
  • Everything in the Supporter tier
  • Funds a 3D printer for demos
  • Logo in the lessons you fund, plus recognition in those lessons
  • A dedicated thank you post on Instagram
Most impact $2,500+ Partner
  • Everything in the Builder tier
  • Funds a new lesson track built from scratch
  • Named recognition on the lesson track it creates
  • Instagram collab post, plus tags in posts about your lessons
  • Invitation to attend a session

In kind sponsorship counts too. Donated hardware, filament, surplus components, meeting space, or transportation help just as much as cash, sometimes more. There is a field for this in the form below.
